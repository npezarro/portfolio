Foodie

Public AI restaurant and food recommendation app. Opinionated recs (restaurants, cafes, boba, matcha) backed by real ratings from Google, Yelp, Reddit, and editorial sources, filtered by vibe, price, dietary needs, and distance. Same architecture as Shopper: an async job queue routes queries to a Docker-containerized Claude CLI, isolated on an alt account for untrusted public input.
